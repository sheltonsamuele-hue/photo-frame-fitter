Known limits
  • Sony RAW (.ARW) opens on the PC version when the camera saved it as uncompressed RAW (the a7 II does). The file is converted at full sensor resolution using the camera's white balance and a standard color profile. Colors are close to the camera's JPEG but not identical, and lens corrections (distortion, vignetting) aren't applied. Compressed ARWs, and ARWs on iPhone, aren't supported.
  • Other camera RAW files (.CR3, .NEF, .DNG and others) can't be opened. Export them from your photo software as JPEG (highest quality) or PNG first. Don't use TIFF on Windows: Edge and Chrome can't open it.
  • HEIC photos open in iPhone Safari but usually not in Edge or Chrome on Windows. On the iPhone, set Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, or share the photo as JPEG.
  • TIFF opens in iPhone Safari only. Edge and Chrome on Windows can't open it, so export as JPEG (highest quality) or PNG instead.
  • Color: output is sRGB. Wide-gamut (Display P3) photos may shift slightly in very saturated colors.
  • Quality: photo pixels are never resized. Print size is set with DPI metadata, so a low-resolution photo gets a warning, not upscaling.
  • iPhone memory: Safari can't draw images larger than about 16.7 megapixels. Larger outputs are refused, never shrunk. Use the PC version for those.
  • iPhone photo picker: if the picker shows a size option, choose Actual Size.
  • EXIF camera data is kept in JPEG output from JPEG originals. PNG output has no EXIF.
